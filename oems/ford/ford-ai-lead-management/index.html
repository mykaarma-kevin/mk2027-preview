FAQ

Your Ford program questions answered

Is myKaarma an approved AI lead management provider for Ford?
Yes. myKaarma is an approved Ford preferred partner for AI lead management, including our Service Concierge and Service Pre-Diag features.
Does the AI replace my BDC?
No. Service Concierge handles the calls your team doesn't reach and the questions that don't need a person. Service Concierge uses the owner's VIN and other information to provide relevant answers. It escalates anything it shouldn't handle, and your reporting shows how often that happens.
What does Service Pre-Diag actually collect?
Service Pre-Diag uses vehicle information and service history to ask relevant questions and provide specific answers and options for the customer's vehicle. It interviews the customer about their vehicle, asks follow-up questions, and can collect photos or video. That gets combined with vehicle history, parts availability, and shop availability before the appointment is booked.
Can we claim this through Ford co-op?
Service Connect, Service Concierge and Service Pre-Diag may be eligible, schedule a demo and ask us about the current claim process for your store.
Can we talk with dealers currently using myKaarma?
Yes. Request a demo and ask for references.
Are myKaarma features co-op eligible?
myKaarma Service Concierge, Service Pre-Diag, and ServiceConnect may be eligible for co-op funding, schedule a demo to learn more.
