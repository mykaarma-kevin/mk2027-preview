How Valet Service works with myKaarma

1

Configuring Valet Service

myKaarma’s Scheduler+ and Pickup and Dropoff tools give your dealerships fine-grained control over when and where Valet Service is offered along with driver tracking, valet scheduling and reporting to ensure things are working correctly.

2

Booking and Scheduling

Your customers can schedule repairs and service using the embedded myKaarma Scheduler+ tool from your dealership website. ROs are routed to the correct store and vehicle service history and VIN data are populated automatically to offer the right service options, find recalls and sync with NCAR.

3

Valet Pickup

Your customers get live notifications and updates about their valet’s arrival along with a map view and text messages for a painless, trusted pickup experience.

4

Repair and Dropoff

Service moves faster with quicker approvals and clearer customer communication all powered by myKaarma. When vehicles are complete they can be dropped off immediately so your service lane keeps moving while providing a luxury customer experience.

FAQ

Your Nissan service lane technology questions answered

Does myKaarma work with NCAR?
Yes - myKaarma, including Scheduler+, works with Nissan's NCAR program for repairs, warranty, recall, myNissan app, and other integrations.
How can my dealership customers book service?
myKaarma Scheduler integrates directly with your DMS for real-time availability and updates for repair orders, parts, schedules and more. You can send personalized links to customers that let's them schedule service without repetitive form fills for their contact and vehicle information.
Can I talk with some dealers currently using myKaarma?
Absolutely! We want to create an environment for your team to learn from other successful groups and dealerships. To connect with other dealers currently using myKaarma, simply set a demo with our team and ask for references you can reach out to.
