FAQ

Your Nissan service lane technology questions answered

Does myKaarma work with NCAR?
Yes - myKaarma, including Scheduler+, works with Nissan's NCAR program for repairs, warranty, recall, myNissan app, and other integrations.
How can my dealership customers book service?
myKaarma Scheduler integrates directly with your DMS for real-time availability and updates for repair orders, parts, schedules and more. You can send personalized links to customers that let's them schedule service without repetitive form fills for their contact and vehicle information.
Can I talk with some dealers currently using myKaarma?
Absolutely! We want to create an environment for your team to learn from other successful groups and dealerships. To connect with other dealers currently using myKaarma, simply set a demo with our team and ask for references you can reach out to.
