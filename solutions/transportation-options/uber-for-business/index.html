FAQ

About our Uber for business integration

How does payment for Uber rides work?
Your Uber ride cost will be added to your monthly myKaarma bill, simplifying accounting and reducing the number of vendors you need to pay monthly.
Can customers track their rides to see where the driver is?
Yes, the customer will have the ability to track the driver so they can see when they get close and easily communicate with their driver if they’re struggling to locate them.
Does myKaarma have a mobile app?
Yes, myKaarma has a mobile app that allows for you to manage communications, check in customers, complete multi-point inspections, take videos, and more!
