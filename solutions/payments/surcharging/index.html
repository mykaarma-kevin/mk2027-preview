Frequently Asked Questions

What is a dealership credit card surcharge?

A credit card surcharge is a disclosed fee added when a customer uses an eligible credit card. It helps the dealership offset some or all of its cost for accepting that payment, subject to applicable limits.

Can a dealership surcharge debit or prepaid cards?

No. Card-network rules prohibit surcharges on debit and prepaid cards, including debit cards processed without a personal identification number. The payment workflow must distinguish those cards from eligible credit cards.

How should the surcharge be disclosed?

Applicable card-network rules require clear disclosure before the transaction and separate identification of the surcharge on the receipt. Additional requirements may apply based on the dealership’s locations and payment channels.

Will surcharging affect the customer experience?

Clear disclosure gives your customer a chance to understand the surcharge and select another accepted payment method when available.

How will my team see all the details of the completed payments?
Your team will automatically receive a report every day showing all of the payments done via myKaarma. They will also have the ability to manually pull payment reconciliation reporting giving your accounting office the ability to streamline their end-of-month operations.

Are your payment tools secure?
Yes, our payment tools are built with cutting-edge PCI security technology to keep your customers’ information safe from bad actors. We also protect your dealership from potential fraudulent chargebacks using tools like 3DSecure. Book time with our experts to learn more!
