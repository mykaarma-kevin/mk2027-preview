SOLUTIONS FOR ACCOUNTING

A proven path to business performance

Simplify your customer experience, provide more payment options, and make reconciliation easy with Payments and Surcharging.
Request a Demo

Painless payments, compliance without compromises

Provide your customers a variety of ways to pay across all departments at your dealership while realizing more revenue by decreasing transaction costs, securing against chargebacks, and streamlining your end-of-month reconciliation.

Make payments a part of your business growth

Stop letting an antiquated payments system slow your business and take your revenue. Use myKaarma Payments and Surcharging to handle all business, improve end-of-month processes, and grow your revenue.

  • Reconciliation made easy
    Be confident in your cash flow; receive automatically delivered reports daily to make reconciliation painless.
  • Compliant surcharging
    Stop losing revenue to credit card fees; pass on transaction fees to payers to reduce operational costs.
  • Defense against chargebacks
    Verify to shift responsibility; 3D Secure verifies customer identity to pass chargeback responsibility to the bank.
  • Dispute management
    Have some help; our experts will assist your team to ensure disputes are handled and closed.
  • State-of-the-art terminals
    Mobile and flexible; our mobile terminals allow you to take payment anywhere in the dealership from any myKaarma user.
  • Online payments
    Pay from your phone; send payment invoices via text and email for customer convenience.

Your team will automatically receive a report every day showing all of the payments done via myKaarma. They will also have the ability to manually pull payment reconciliation reporting giving your accounting office the ability to streamline their end-of-month operations.

Are your payment tools secure?
Yes, we have built our payment tools with cutting edge security technology keeping your customers' information safe from potential bad actors. We also protect your dealerships from potential fraud chargebacks through tools like 3DSecure. Book some time with our experts to learn more!
