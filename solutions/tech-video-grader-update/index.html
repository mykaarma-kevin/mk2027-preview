Better technician videos. More confident customers.

Tech Video Grader uses AI to review MPI video quality and turn dealership priorities into practical coaching—helping your team explain findings clearly and customers make informed service decisions.

See the grade. Know what to coach.

Get a clear view of what a technician video shows, what it explains, and where it can improve. Tech Video Grader brings scores and analysis into the myKaarma workflow, so the next coaching conversation starts with something specific.

Your Tech Video Grader questions, answered.

What does Tech Video Grader evaluate?
Tech Video Grader evaluates video communication and inspection coverage, including introductions, vehicle checks, value-building elements, and overall delivery. It helps identify what the video shows and explains; it does not replace a technician’s mechanical inspection.
Does grading delay video delivery?
No. Video processing and delivery keep moving while grading runs in the background, so the customer conversation does not have to wait for a grade.
Can grading reflect our dealership’s standards?
Yes. Grading can be customized around dealership priorities and OEM guidelines. Ask for a demonstration of the configuration options available for your workflow.
Where can our team see feedback?
Video Grader analysis is available within ServiceCart, with dealership reporting in myIQ. Technicians can also view a seven-day performance snapshot on the Route Sheet; that snapshot refreshes every two hours.
Which videos are analyzed?
The current product guide specifies the first video on each repair order. Record one complete inspection video per repair order so the analysis can reflect the full explanation.
How can we track improvement?
Use the technician leaderboard and monthly reporting to recognize progress, identify repeated gaps, and guide coaching over time.
